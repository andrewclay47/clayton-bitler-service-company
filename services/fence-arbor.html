Arbor Construction

Arbors, pergolas & trellises

Whether you need privacy or simply want to add appeal, we can design and build an arbor, pergola, or trellis to fit your needs — transforming your backyard into a functionally beautiful outdoor space.

What is an arbor?

An arbor is an overhead structure that makes the outdoors more inviting, extends livable space, creates a shady retreat, and serves as a major landscaping element. We help you create an environment that reflects your personality and style.

  • Custom arbor design and build
  • Pergolas and trellises
  • Privacy and curb-appeal solutions
  • Structures that extend your outdoor living space
