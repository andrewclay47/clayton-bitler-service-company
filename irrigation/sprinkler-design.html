Professional CAD Designs

Sprinkler Genius provides sprinkler designs for our installs, as well as stand-alone sprinkler designs for other businesses and homeowners. Every plan is drawn to scale in CAD with the calculations and watering schedule included.

Design details and requirements

Our Designs

What you receive

  • Professional CAD designs drawn by experienced irrigation designers
  • Your choice of scaling so the plan matches how you work
  • All calculations shown on the drawing — nothing hidden
  • Watering schedule calculated and sent along with the design

What We Need

To get started on your plan

  • 1

    A proper site plan with measurements

    If one isn’t available, we can survey the property for you.

  • 2

    Material preference

    Any specific brands or products you want specified.

  • 3

    Water meter size, location, and static pressure

    We can gather this on site as well.

  • 4

    Digital photos

    Helpful, but optional.

  • 5

    Company name, phone number, and email address

    So we can send the finished design back to you.
