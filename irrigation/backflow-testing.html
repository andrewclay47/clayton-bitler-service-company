Backflow Testing

Annual backflow testing helps ensure that only clean, potable water is flowing through your plumbing into your home or business.

Licensed Backflow Experts

There are several different types of backflow prevention devices in use, and Sprinkler Genius’s licensed backflow experts are familiar with all of them. No matter what type of device your home or business has installed, we can handle all of your backflow testing, maintenance, repair, and replacement needs.

Requirements

Who Needs a Backflow Test?

All commercial and many residential sprinkler systems need their backflow device tested annually.

Commercial Property

Most Texas businesses are required by the state to have an up-to-date backflow prevention device installed. Commercial property in Texas should have an annual backflow test conducted by our trained and certified backflow testing experts.

Residential Homes

It’s a good idea for residential homes to have the backflow device tested periodically. This is especially true of older homes, which often do not have an adequate backflow prevention device installed.

Notified by the City?

In many cases the Water and Sewer Department will contact you and require a backflow test. This isn’t cause for alarm — it’s a routine procedure to make sure no contaminated water is entering your water supply from outside sources.

Fast, Certified Testing

In most cases, Sprinkler Genius can conduct your backflow test in 15–20 minutes. If problems are detected, we have the equipment and material on hand to handle most simple maintenance and repairs.

If you need a new backflow prevention device installed — either to replace a defective one or because one was never installed, which is usually only the case with older construction — we can handle that too.

  • Annual testing and certification
  • Maintenance and repair of existing devices
  • New backflow preventer installation and replacement
  • All types of backflow prevention devices

Not a DIY Job

Backflow testing is not a job for do-it-yourselfers. When you need this test, it must be conducted by someone who is trained and certified in your area. If you live in North Texas, Sprinkler Genius offers the best, most qualified licensed professionals to handle your backflow test, certification, maintenance, and repair needs.
