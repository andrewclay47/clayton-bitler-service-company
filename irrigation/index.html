About Sprinkler Genius

Sprinkler Genius has over 15 years of experience servicing, repairing, and installing lawn sprinkler systems in North Texas. We are a locally owned and operated company that provides prompt, professional, and courteous service.

Our irrigators and technicians are licensed by the state of Texas and offer a wide range of services that will keep your lawn green and healthy. We use only the highest quality parts that come with manufacturer’s warranties.

Our goal is to create a long-lasting relationship with all of our customers. We will get the job done quickly and correctly the first time.
