Custom Sprinkler Schedules

Watering your landscape with an automatic irrigation system will likely be the single largest use of water in your home. Proper irrigation scheduling is a skill that surprisingly few have mastered. Many people don’t realize that they must change their irrigation program regularly as the seasons change.

Ideally you should program your sprinkler clock weekly or even daily to maximize efficiency. But even monthly changes to the irrigation schedule will result in substantial water savings and improved plant health. Few people have the time or inclination to take this micro-managing approach — but don’t worry, we can help.

We can dramatically improve efficiency by creating a custom irrigation schedule for your sprinkler system.

How We Build Your Schedule

Five Factors We Take Into Consideration

Every custom schedule is built around the unique conditions of your property and your local watering rules.

1

Soil Type

Clay, loam, or sand — each holds and releases water differently, so run times and cycle counts are adjusted accordingly.

2

Type of Spray

Pop-up heads, rotors, or drip irrigation each apply water at different rates and need their own schedule settings.

3

Zone Types

Sun, shade, and partial shade zones don’t need the same amount of water — we program each zone for its exposure.

4

Plant Material

Grass, seasonal color, native plants, and shrubs all have different water needs throughout the year.

5

Water Restrictions

We factor in watering days and time limits for your city or utility so your schedule stays compliant.
