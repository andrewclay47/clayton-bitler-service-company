Our Installation Process

From Consultation to Walk-Through

Five clear steps, so you always know what’s happening in your yard.

  1. 1

    Meeting

    We begin with a personal consultation to get to know more about what you want and need.

  2. 2

    Design

    Taking your needs into account, we create a design using a professional irrigation CAD program. This master plan is based on water source pressure, hardscapes, and landscape locations. Many companies use excellent products, but the irrigation design is what really counts when it comes to a system that works effectively.

  3. 3

    Installation

    The labor portion begins. We trench where the pipe, valves, heads, and wires will be buried, install the backflow device, and connect all pipes, fittings, valves, wires, and the timer.

  4. 4

    Programming

    We program the controller based on watering days available by the city, the sprinkler requirements for each zone, monthly adjustments, and watering times.

  5. 5

    Walk-Through

    Once installation is complete, we go over the system and explain how to operate the controller. You’ll be given an as-built design showing where heads and valves are located.
