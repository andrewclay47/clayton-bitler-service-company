Owner & Operator

Clayton “Clay” Bitler

Clayton Bitler Service Company is owned and operated by Clayton Bitler. Raised in Richardson, Texas, Clay graduated from nearby Berkner High School and earned a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration from The University of Texas at Dallas (UTD).

He currently lives in Murphy, TX with his wife and their two kids — still serving the communities he grew up in and around.

  • Licensed Irrigator — LI 8729
